# Progression

<!-- Normative authority: specs/007-tech-progression/spec.md -->

You do not research bigger numbers. You unlock new ways for the ship to
work, and each one obsoletes the ship you built.

1. **Tier 1: Mechanical Grinding.** Coal and wood, manual routing,
   brute-force hull smashing. You are the logistics network.
2. **Tier 2: Chemical and Thermal.** Oil and methane processing,
   conveyor belts, thermal lances that melt ice before impact. Flow
   replaces hauling; heat becomes an economy.
3. **Tier 3: Autonomous and Nuclear.** Reactors, full logistics
   automation, localized AI control modules, sonic resonance
   shattering. The ship becomes a self-regulating organism; you stop
   operating and start governing.

A routing system that worked for coal will bottleneck fatally on
nuclear. The refit is the game.

Blueprints come from the world: shipwrecks in pack ice, and the deep
interiors of glacial walls and Iceberg Nodes. Big progression is always
paid for with a risk taken outside.
