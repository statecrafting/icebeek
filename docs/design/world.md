# The world

<!-- Normative authority: specs/006-world-and-expeditions/spec.md -->

A procedurally generated frozen ocean, blanked out by the **Fog of
Winter**. Sight is never free: scouts, sensor power, or risky proximity
buy every revealed tile.

**The ice**, in three bands:

- *Pancake ice*: easy passage, thin pickings. Breathing room.
- *Pack ice*: real resistance, and trapped shipwrecks worth cracking
  open. The bread-and-butter gamble.
- *Glacial walls*: monuments. Breaching one takes massive momentum or
  advanced prow tech, and the reward is ancient, tier-gating
  blueprints.

**Iceberg Nodes.** Sometimes the fog parts around something huge: a
frozen skyscraper, a dormant volcano. You anchor, and anchoring means
stopping: the surrounding ice starts shifting shut while your crawler
rovers strip-mine the node. Every extra minute of greed is bought with
hull stress and a narrower escape.

**Weather that fights dirty.** Super-storms freeze your intake valves
and force manual overrides. Solar flares scramble drone logic, and the
players who survive them are the ones who kept dumb mechanical belts
running under the smart network.
