# The core loop

<!-- Normative authority: specs/003-core-gameplay-loop/spec.md -->

Three phases, one spiral:

1. **Break.** Pick a line through the ice. Thick ice pays better and
   costs more: fuel, prow wear, hull stress.
2. **Ingest.** The grind feeds you. Scrap, ancient tech, biomass, and
   ice pour through the intake valves into the cargo hold while you
   move.
3. **Build.** Route what you caught: refineries, fabricators, the
   engine core. Automate it so it runs while you look away.

The spiral: every pass leaves the ship heavier, hungrier, and more
capable. And underneath it all, the clock: **if the ship stops, the ice
closes and crushes the hull.** Fuel starvation, a cold core, a dead
prow, a gridlocked cargo bay: all of them are just different ways of
stopping.
