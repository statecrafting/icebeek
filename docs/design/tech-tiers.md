# Tech mechanisms

<!-- Normative authority: specs/016-tech-tiers/spec.md -->

Progression runs on two currencies. Research accrues only when a
Refinery processes ancient technology out of the hold: no passive
trickle, no clock. Blueprints come from the world as the Salvage
event family (wreck salvage in pack ice, wall caches behind glacial
breaches, node vaults at expeditions), so every major unlock is paid
for with a Macro risk decision. Duplicates convert to research;
the blueprint set never double-counts.

A tier gates what can exist: rooms on the grid, prow tracks, rule
vocabulary. Gated orders are rejected like any invalid build. What a
tier does not do is delete: old equipment keeps running, and the
paradigm rule bites through economics instead, because advancement
atomically swaps the ship onto the new tier's fuel, heat, and
throughput profile. A tier-1 layout inside a tier-2 economy is
legal, running, and visibly obsolete: the refit itch is the game.
