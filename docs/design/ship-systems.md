# The ship

<!-- Normative authority: specs/004-ship-systems/spec.md -->

A multi-decked behemoth with four organs:

- **The Prow.** Your interface to the ice. Kinetic rams for pack ice,
  thermal lances that melt a path, sonic disruptors that shatter
  glacial rock. Each track eats power and sheds heat differently, and
  all of them wear out: the prow repair line never closes.
- **The Engine Core.** The heart. It demands an uninterrupted feed of
  refined fuel; if core temperature drops, systems brown out one by
  one, farthest first, until propulsion dies and the crush clock runs.
- **The Internal Grid.** Node-based rooms (foundries, hydroponics,
  drone bays, heat sinks) joined by a logistical spine: belts for
  solids, pipes for fluids, data lines for signals. Deck space is
  finite; tearing out a working layout to build a better one is normal
  play.
- **Heat.** Machinery overheats, biology freezes, and coolant is just
  melted ice with a job. Route exhaust heat to the greenhouse and it is
  free warmth; vent it overboard and it is waste.
